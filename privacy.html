Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 22, 2026 · Last updated: July 22, 2026

APP Merchant Services ("we," "us," or "our"), operated by Derek Fite in Palm Coast, Florida, provides payment processing and point-of-sale solutions to businesses across the United States. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through this website, how we use and share it, and the choices you have. It is written for the business owners and operators who contact us — not for consumers or patients.

1. Who This Policy Covers

This site markets payment processing services to businesses. The information we collect is business contact information and details about your business — not patient information and not medical information. If you are an individual consumer, or a patient of one of the businesses we serve, this policy is not directed at you, and you should not send us personal or medical information.

2. We Are Not a Healthcare Provider

Many of the businesses we work with operate in or near healthcare — online pharmacies, telehealth platforms, and similar. To be clear about our own role: APP Merchant Services is not a healthcare provider, not a pharmacy, and not a HIPAA covered entity or business associate. This website is not intended to collect protected health information (PHI).

Please do not submit patient names, prescription details, diagnoses, or any other medical or health information through any form, email, or other channel on this site. This site is for business and merchant-account information only.

If health information is submitted to us anyway, we will delete it once we identify it, and we will not use it for any purpose.

We also do not collect merchant underwriting documents — such as licenses, bank statements, or government-issued IDs — through this website. Never use a form or email on this site to send card numbers, Social Security numbers, bank account credentials, or passwords. When documents like these are needed to open a merchant account, we will arrange a secure channel for them separately.

3. Information We Collect

Information you provide

When you submit a contact or pre-qualification form, or contact us directly, we collect:

  • Your name
  • Business name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • The answers in our pre-qualification form about your business model, licensing, certifications, and how you operate

These pre-qualification answers describe your business — its licenses, certifications, and operations — not any patient or individual.

Information collected automatically

When you visit the site, some information is collected automatically through standard web technologies:

  • IP address
  • Device and browser type
  • Pages visited and referring links
  • Cookies and similar identifiers, including security and bot-detection cookies

4. Service Providers and Tools We Use

We rely on the following third-party providers to run the site and respond to inquiries. Each processes data only as needed to provide its service, under its own privacy terms:

  • Jotform — hosts our contact and pre-qualification forms.
  • Google Analytics (GA4) — measures anonymous website traffic and usage patterns.
  • Cloudflare — content delivery, website security, and bot detection (sets security cookies).
  • Hostinger — website hosting.
  • Google Workspace — our business email.

We do not currently use advertising or retargeting pixels (for example, a Meta/Facebook pixel). If that ever changes, we will update this policy first.

5. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your inquiry
  • Evaluate your pre-qualification and eligibility for a merchant account
  • Follow up with you about our services, including occasional marketing
  • Operate, secure, and improve the site

6. How We Share Your Information

When you submit a contact or pre-qualification form, the information you provide may be shared with our payment processing partners and their sponsor banks for the purpose of evaluating your eligibility and, if you move forward, establishing a merchant account. This is the core purpose of the pre-qualification process. We describe these partners generically here; the specific processor and bank are identified to you directly when you begin an application.

We also share information with the service providers listed above, who process it on our behalf. We may disclose information if required by law, regulation, or valid legal process, or to protect our rights, safety, or property.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for targeted advertising or for their own marketing purposes.

7. Cookies and Your Choices

We use cookies for analytics (understanding how the site is used) and for security (protecting the site from bots and abuse). You can control or block cookies through your browser settings; blocking some cookies may affect how the site works.

Some browsers can send a Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. Because we do not sell personal information or share it for targeted advertising, there is currently nothing for a GPC signal to opt out of on our site. If our practices ever change, we will treat GPC signals as valid opt-out requests.

8. Communications and Consent

When you submit a form, you consent to be contacted by email or phone about your inquiry. If we send marketing emails, every one includes an unsubscribe link, and we honor opt-outs promptly, as required by the CAN-SPAM Act.

We do not send marketing text messages. If we ever add SMS follow-up, we will ask for your separate, express written consent first, as required by the TCPA — we will not add you to text messages based on this policy alone.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, state privacy laws may give you rights over your personal information. Our business falls below the size thresholds that make most of these state laws mandatory for us. Even so, we honor the following requests from residents of any state:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Delete your information
  • Opt out of marketing communications

To make a request, email us at derek@appmerchantservices.com. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

10. Data Retention

We keep form submissions and related correspondence for 24 months, after which we delete or anonymize them, unless we need to keep them longer to meet a legal obligation or resolve a dispute.

11. How We Protect Your Information

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. The site is served over encrypted HTTPS connections through Cloudflare, and access to inquiry data is limited to people who need it. No method of transmitting or storing data is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

12. Data Breach Notification

If a data breach affects your personal information, we will notify you and the appropriate authorities as required by applicable state law.

13. Children's Privacy

This site is directed to business owners and operators. It is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If you believe a minor has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

14. Links to Other Sites

Our site may link to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those sites; their own policies apply.

15. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the effective date at the top of this page. Your continued use of the site after an update means you accept the revised policy.
