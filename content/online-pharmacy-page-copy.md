## SECTION: The compliance stack (this is the SEO and credibility core of the page)

**H2:** You already met the industry's standards. We speak that language.

Online pharmacy is one of the most carefully regulated corners of American commerce, and the requirements come from every direction at once. Here is the stack we review with you, and what each piece means for your merchant account.

**H3: State pharmacy board licensing**
Every pharmacy needs a license from its home state board, plus nonresident licenses in the states it ships into. Multistate licensure in good standing is the foundation of every underwriting file. If you hold it, approval gets dramatically simpler.

**H3: LegitScript certification**
Card networks require card-not-present pharmacy and telehealth merchants to be certified by an approved third party, and LegitScript is the certification the networks and major ad platforms recognize. If you are already certified, you have cleared the single biggest gate in pharmacy underwriting. If you are mid-application, we can work with you in parallel so processing is ready the day your certification clears.

**H3: NABP accreditation and the .pharmacy domain**
The National Association of Boards of Pharmacy runs the accreditation programs (formerly known as VIPPS) that many state boards recognize or require, along with the .pharmacy verified domain. These credentials strengthen an underwriting file and signal exactly the kind of operation banks want to approve.

**H3: DEA registration, only where it applies**
DEA registration governs controlled substances, and only controlled substances. Many online pharmacy models never touch them. GLP-1 medications for weight management, for example, are not controlled substances, which is why pure weight-management telehealth models often have a simpler compliance profile than practices dispensing controlled medications. Knowing the difference matters, because it changes what your underwriting file needs to contain.

**H3: Telemedicine prescribing rules**
Federal law sets the conditions under which a prescription can be issued through a telehealth encounter. Legitimate telehealth platforms build their clinical workflows around these rules, and a clear description of your prescribing model is part of every strong application.

**H3: Card network integrity programs**
The card networks run integrity and risk programs that require acquirers to register and monitor merchants in categories like pharmacy. Registration is an industry-wide requirement that applies no matter who processes your payments. The difference is whether your provider handles it knowledgeably and tells you what to expect up front. We do.

## SECTION: FAQ (FAQPage schema)

**Q: Can an online pharmacy get approved without LegitScript certification?**
Certification is an industry-wide requirement for card-not-present pharmacy processing, so plan on it. If your application is in progress, we can run underwriting in parallel so you are ready to process as soon as certification is issued.

**Q: We were terminated by a previous payment platform. Are we disqualified?**
Not automatically. Terminations from mainstream platforms are common in this category and often reflect the platform's blanket policy rather than anything about your business. Be upfront about it in your eligibility check and we will tell you honestly what it means for your file.

**Q: Do you work with telehealth companies that do not dispense medication themselves?**
Yes. Platforms that connect patients with licensed prescribers and fulfill through licensed pharmacy partners are a core part of this program. Your prescribing and fulfillment model just needs to be clearly documented.

**Q: What about controlled substances?**
Models that include controlled substances require DEA registration and face additional review, which is an industry-wide standard. Models limited to non-controlled medications, including GLP-1 therapies, typically have a simpler path.

**Q: How long does approval take?**
It depends on how complete your file is. Merchants who arrive with licenses and certification in hand move fastest. You will get a realistic timeline at the eligibility stage, not a promise that moves.
